4 YOE Strategic Takeaways

What Changes Because You're Senior, Not Junior

Most roadmaps online are for freshers. At 4 YOE, the game shifts in 6 important ways.

SHIFT 01 · INTERVIEW WEIGHT

System Design = 35-40%, not 15%

At 4 YOE you'll likely face 2 system design rounds (one HLD, one deep-dive). Add 1-2 weeks to system design prep. Your 4 years of real backend experience is GOLD here — most SDE 2 candidates fake it. You won't have to.

SHIFT 02 · CODING DEPTH

LC Medium → Hard, fewer Easy

SDE 2 interviews skip warm-up Easy problems. Expect medium right away, then hard or 2 mediums in 45 min. Stripe and Atlassian especially go straight to LC Hard. Allocate 70% Medium, 25% Hard, 5% Easy in your prep mix.

SHIFT 03 · BEHAVIORAL

Leadership stories, not just teamwork

Behavioral rounds will probe: Did you mentor anyone? Have you led a project? Resolved a technical disagreement? Made a tradeoff call? If you don't have these stories, build them in your current job before October. Every SDE 2 round will ask.

SHIFT 04 · DEPTH ROUND

Past project deep-dive interviews

Many SDE 2 loops include a "tell me about your most complex project" round where they probe for 30-45 min. Pick 2-3 hardest projects from Circuit House. Document architecture, scale, tradeoffs, your specific contributions. Have war stories ready.

SHIFT 05 · NEGOTIATION

Never reveal ₹17.6L · Never accept SDE 1

Recruiters will try to pin your offer to your current pay. Refuse. "I'm interviewing at the SDE 2 level; I'm looking at total comp in the ₹50-65L range based on Levels.fyi." If they offer SDE 1 / junior — that's a step backward at your YOE. Walk away.

SHIFT 06 · YOUR EDGE

Startup SDE 4yr = strong narrative

At a startup with 4 YOE, you've worn many hats: backend + ops + on-call + cross-cloud. This is exactly what product cos want at SDE 2. Lead every interview by framing this: "I've owned X end-to-end, deployed across AWS/GCP/Azure, debugged production at 2am." That's senior signal.

⚠ The Trap to Avoid Some companies (especially when they sense low current pay) will offer you SDE 1 / Junior level at "good Indian salary" like ₹25-30L. This LOOKS like a 50% hike — but it's a career step backward because (a) you'll be peer'd with 0-2 YOE engineers, (b) leveling up takes 18-24 months, (c) future companies will reference this junior level. Always interview AT the level your YOE warrants. Better to wait 2 more months for a Senior offer than to take a junior one.

Trap Avoidance

Don't Make These Mistakes

✗ Solving 50 random LeetCode problems
✓ Solve 30 problems mapped to 16 patterns. Patterns >> problem count.
✗ Reading solutions before trying for 25 min
✓ Struggle is where learning happens. Set a timer. Only peek after.
✗ Skipping the "patterns doc" — feels like extra work
✓ This doc is what you'll review the night before interviews. Build it from Day 1.
✗ Waiting until Week 18 to apply
✓ Start applying Week 15. The pipeline takes 4-8 weeks. October offers = July/August applications.
✗ Telling recruiters "I currently make 17.6L"
✓ Never reveal current pay. Always ask for the band first. "What's the range for this role?"
✗ Accepting the first offer because of relief
✓ Negotiate every offer. 15-25% bumps are normal. They expect it.
✗ Skipping mock interviews because they're awkward
✓ Mocks expose blind spots cheaply. 8 mocks = 1 successful real interview.
✗ Studying DSA in isolation, ignoring system design
✓ At 2-4 yrs experience, system design IS the differentiator. Don't underweight it.
